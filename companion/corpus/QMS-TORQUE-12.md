# QMS-TORQUE-12

## Purpose
Current Plant B torque procedure for fastening critical joints on aero structures. This revision supersedes all prior torque QMS documents at this plant.

## Trigger
A torque NCR on a serialized unit (example SN-4419) in lot L-8819. Trigger is failed torque, not vibration or paint.

## Action
Place a LOT HOLD on the entire lot. Do not write per-serial holds for WIP siblings. Serials already shipped are not WIP hold targets; raise a shipped exception and escalate to the customer. Cite QMS-TORQUE-12.

## Authority
Quality Engineering (QE) approves the lot hold. Mock MOM is the system of record after human approval.
