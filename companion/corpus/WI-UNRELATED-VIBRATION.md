# WI-UNRELATED-VIBRATION

## Purpose
Spindle vibration watchdog. Near-miss for retrieval.

## Trigger
High RMS vibration on CNC.

## Action
Reduce feed. This is a maintenance watchdog, not a quality containment process.

## Authority
Maintenance.
